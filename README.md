# ✦ THE ARCHIVE — Editorial Birthday Tribute

A modern, high-end, editorial birthday tribute website designed with quiet elegance, tasteful typography, genuine care, and a cosmic space galaxy & nebula background.

---

## ✦ Aesthetic & Design Highlights

- **Space Galaxy & Nebula Background**: Cosmic starfields, interstellar nebula clouds, and subtle shooting stars glowing behind the editorial content.
- **Videezy Video Integration**:
  - The site is configured to automatically play `background.mp4` in a seamless fullscreen loop.
  - You can download the video from [Videezy (45555 - Space Galaxy and Nebula)](https://www.videezy.com/abstract/45555-space-galaxy-and-nebula) and drop it into this folder named **`background.mp4`**, OR click **`EDIT ✎`** on the website and select the file directly!
- **Editorial Typography**: Styled with *Cinzel*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, and *Space Mono* for a timeless magazine aesthetic.
- **Cinematic Entrance**: An understated monogram curtain (`ENTER ARCHIVE →`) with smooth dissolve.
- **The Written Note**: Authentic, grounded, well-crafted letter with an editorial drop cap and one-click copy/print options.
- **The Visual Archive (Lookbook)**: Staggered 35mm film frame collage with film timestamps, aspect ratios, clean lightbox viewer, and live drag-and-drop photo uploader.
- **Ambient Sound**: Minimalist, warm ambient synthesizer (calm and relaxing, not arcade chimes).
- **Discreet Customizer**: Click **EDIT ✎** in the top masthead to personalize her name, edition, date, letter, or background video in seconds.

## 🚀 How to Open & View

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Double click [index.html](file:///home/dk/Desktop/BDAY/index.html) in your Desktop folder (`/home/dk/Desktop/BDAY/index.html`) to open it immediately in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

### Option 2: Local Server
In your terminal, run:
```bash
cd ~/Desktop/BDAY
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit:
👉 **`http://localhost:8000`**
